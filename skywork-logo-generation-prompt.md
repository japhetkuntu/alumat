# Logo Generation Brief: "AlumUnion" (for Skywork.ai)

## What this is

Generate a primary logo for **AlumUnion** — a multi-tenant SaaS platform that alumni associations and universities license to run their own branded alumni portal (member networking, dues/contributions, events, job board, mentorship, class notes). AlumUnion is the platform operator's own brand — the name that appears on the marketing site, billing, the platform-operator admin console, and login footers — separate from and not to be confused with any individual university's own branding (each licensed institution white-labels the member-facing app with its own logo and color).

**Generate 5 distinct logo concepts** so I can choose one to move forward with. Each concept should be a genuinely different idea/direction — not 5 minor color or font variations of the same mark.

## Brand meaning to encode

Pick from these associations (don't try to cram all of them into one mark — different concepts can lean on different ones):
- **Union / connection** — bringing separate individuals (graduates scattered across careers and countries) into one coordinated network
- **Continuity after graduation** — the relationship with one's institution doesn't end at commencement, it continues
- **Trust and infrastructure** — this is the software backbone an institution runs its official alumni relations on; it should read as credible, stable, and professional enough for a university to put its name next to
- **Multiplicity of institutions** — one platform, many alumni bodies — a subtle nod to "many becoming one system" is welcome but optional

## What to avoid

- Nothing that reads as tied to one specific university (no generic mortarboard-and-diploma cliché as the *only* idea across all 5 — at most one concept may explore an academic motif; the other 4 should not)
- No literal globe-with-dots "connected world" stock-icon look
- Nothing overly playful/consumer — this is B2B software sold to university administrations, not a students' social app
- No gradients that would break when reproduced in a single flat color (see requirements below)
- No text-only wordmark with zero mark/symbol — every concept needs an actual symbol, even if minimal

## Wordmark

- Name to render: **AlumUnion** (single word, capital A and capital U — do not split into "Alum Union" as two words)
- Typeface should feel geometric-sans and confident — legible at small sizes (favicon, browser tab, mobile app icon), not a display/script face
- Provide the logo in two lockups per concept: (1) symbol + wordmark side-by-side (primary, horizontal), and (2) symbol alone (for use as a square app icon / favicon)

## Technical requirements

- Must work as a **single flat color** (pure black and pure white versions) — no reliance on gradients or multi-color blending to read correctly, since it needs to work reversed-out on dark backgrounds and printed in one spot color
- Must remain legible and distinct at **24×24px** (browser favicon size) as the symbol-alone version
- Square/contained symbol so it crops cleanly into a rounded-square app-icon shape
- Deliver on a transparent or plain white background, no shadows or 3D bevel effects baked into the mark

## Output requested

- **5 distinct concepts**, each shown as: full-color version, single-color black version, single-color white-on-dark version, and the symbol-alone icon crop
- One-line rationale under each concept explaining the idea behind it
