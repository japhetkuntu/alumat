## Brand & Style

The Institution Portal is a calm, precise institutional operations workspace. No product brand name is assumed yet — the sidebar leads with the institution's own name and identifies itself only as "Institution Portal," ready for a real product name later. Use a light slate page field with white low-elevation panels and crisp slate dividers; deep ink headings; restrained #2563EB for primary actions, selected navigation, and links; amber and red are reserved for payment or moderation attention. Inter is compact and highly legible. Controls have 6-8px radii, small status badges, deliberate empty space between operational zones, and table-first density. Persistent dark-ink institution rail identifies the active tenant, user role, and assigned scope. Avoid decorative illustration, oversized hero copy, gradients, glass panels, or nested cards. Show accountable activity through queue counts, exact status chips, timestamps, audit trails, plain-language consequences, and explicit SuperAdmin-only lock states.
