The Reality

Every association we talk to is fighting the same three problems.

Not a lack of features — a lack of one place where alumni, records and money all live together.

01

Your alumni are scattered

A WhatsApp group for one year group. A Facebook group nobody moderates. A spreadsheet that's two executives out of date. Different year groups, different channels, no single place any of it lives — so a little more of the network quietly disappears every year.

→
Fixed by: one searchable Directory organised by year group automatically, plus in-app, email and SMS Notifications that actually reach people — instead of dying in a WhatsApp group.
DirectoryBatchesNotificationsCommunities
02

Nobody knows who your alumni actually are

Ask "how many alumni do we have, and who are they?" and the honest answer is a guess, a headcount from years ago, or a folder of screenshots. There's no reliable, verified, searchable record of who your alumni actually are.

→
Fixed by: a verified member database, searchable by year, program and location, with rich profiles — not a spreadsheet someone's cousin made.
Verified ProfilesSearch & FiltersSchool RecordsBusiness Directory
03

Mobilising alumni for money is painful

You need GHS 100 from 500 alumni. Someone drafts a WhatsApp broadcast. People pay however they can and send screenshots as proof. The treasurer reconciles every one by hand. Two weeks in, someone asks "how much have we raised?" — and the honest answer is "let me check."

→
Fixed by: built-in campaigns and dues with real payment collection — reconciled automatically, the moment it clears, with a live total instead of a guess.
